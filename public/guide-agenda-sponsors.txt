## 5. Session types overview

re:Invent 2026 has 2,200+ sessions, about 70% of them interactive, across twelve session formats. The core formats a concierge will be asked about:

- **Breakout sessions** — Lecture-style 60-minute presentations on architecture, best practices, and deep dives, led by AWS experts, customers, and partners. Recorded and posted to the AWS Events YouTube channel a few days later; walk-up seating.
- **Chalk talks** — Highly interactive 60-minute sessions with smaller audiences; technical deep dives with whiteboard architecture discussions and audience questions. Not recorded; reserve early — popular ones book out fast.
- **Workshops** — Two-hour hands-on sessions where you work through tasks in live AWS accounts in a small team; bring a laptop. Not recorded.
- **Builders' sessions** — 60-minute small-group sessions where an AWS expert guides you through building a solution to a technical problem. Not recorded.
- **Code talks** — 60-minute live-coding sessions where AWS experts build solutions in real time.
- **Lightning talks** — 20-minute talks on a single focused idea or demo, held in the Venetian Expo Hall and the Wynn/Encore Upper Convention Promenade; walk-up only, no reservations.

Other formats in the catalog include self-paced labs, gamified learning, exam prep sessions, and bootcamps (certification prep; registered attendees get 50% off AWS Certification exams). Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/compute/your-serverless-attendees-guide-to-aws-reinvent-2026/, https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/networking-and-content-delivery/your-aws-networking-and-content-delivery-guide-to-reinvent-2026/, and https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/sessions/how-youll-learn/

## 7. Practical facts a concierge gets asked

- **Pass:** Full conference pass $2,499 (includes keynotes, sessions, workshops, labs, the Expo, and re:Play); 10% off when buying 10 or more passes in one transaction. An AWS Builder ID is required to register. Source: https://agentconfs.com/articles/aws-reinvent-2026-guide (checked against aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent on Nov 5, 2026)
- **Virtual:** Keynotes stream free online; 50+ sessions are also streamed with free livestream registration. Source: https://agentconfs.com/articles/aws-reinvent-2026-guide
- **Badge pickup tip:** Pick up at the airport on arrival (Terminals 1 and 3, open until 11:59 PM Sunday and Monday); bring your registration QR code and photo ID. Source: https://builder.aws.com/content/3MVzlWfFKrdSe2Gjc8eoxmQsUGX
- **Getting around:** Free conference shuttles link the venues during conference hours (live times and stops in the AWS Events app); the Las Vegas Monorail is free with an attendee badge. Allow 30–45 minutes between activities at different venues. Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/agenda/ and https://www.fractivo.com/reinvent?src=home-footer
- **AWS Events app:** Carries your schedule, reserved seating, indoor maps/wayfinding, and shuttle details; sign in with your Builder ID. Source: https://www.watchpreviously.com/news/aws-reinvent-2026-las-vegas-guide/
- **Meals:** Breakfast and lunch are served on full conference days; the Tuesday/Wednesday networking receptions and re:Play include food and drinks. Sources: https://builder.aws.com/content/3M1bvIGN9QJ3dxY4T2RSsDaB9EL/aws-reinvent-the-attendee-playbook-ai-track-ai-economics and https://www.fractivo.com/reinvent?src=home-footer
- **Caesars Forum extras:** Caesars Forum hosts a help desk, the Customer Showcase, and the AWS Certification Lounge. A recurring daily agenda block runs 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM at Caesars Forum (Mon–Thu); the agenda page's text listing did not capture that block's title. Source: https://www.fractivo.com/reinvent?src=home-footer and https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/agenda/
- **Thursday security focus:** re:Inforce, the former standalone AWS security conference, is integrated into re:Invent for 2026, with Thursday as the security focus day at Wynn. Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/agenda/ and https://lasvegaswonders.com/aws-reinvent-las-vegas/

- **Headline sponsor names on AWS's own pages:** The AWS homepage names only the Emerald tier in text (the sponsor names render as logos); the Emerald and Diamond name lists above come from the official sponsor catalog as captured by data-conference-database (Nov 15, 2026), cross-checked against confhunt.com's Emerald list.
