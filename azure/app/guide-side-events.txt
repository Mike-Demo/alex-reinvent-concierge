# AWS re:Invent 2026 — Side Events Research

**Date researched:** November 25, 2026
**Conference:** AWS re:Invent 2026, Las Vegas, Mon Nov 30 – Fri Dec 4, 2026
**Scope:** Parties, concerts, receptions, socials, and partner/community events outside the main conference program. A few official AWS social events (Expo reception, re:Play) are included because attendees consistently treat them as part of the social calendar.

## Notes, discrepancies, and things that could NOT be verified

- **Friday, Dec 4 is thin.** Aggregators list only a handful of Friday side events, and several (e.g., "Breakfast – Final Connections," "Dinner – The Next Move" on Echelon) show "Venue TBD" with no public RSVP detail beyond the aggregator listing, so they were not given full entries.
- **Aggregator disagreements exist.** Examples: the FinOps Leaders Dinner venue (Delmonico Steakhouse per Echelon/Conference Parties vs. just "The Venetian Resort" per Exa); the Rootly Kickoff Party venue (Rockhouse per Echelon vs. "shared with confirmed guests" per Exa); GitHub Social Club end time (4 PM per Exa vs. 5 PM per Echelon); the HabileLabs Kickoff Night start (6 PM per Echelon/Exa vs. 7 PM per Raidan Dev). Entries follow the majority source.
- **Pier Racing 2026** (Sun, Nov 29, 6–10 PM, SpeedVegas, hosted by Pier Cloud) is real and listed everywhere, but Exa reported registration and the waitlist already closed, so it was left out of the main list.
- **Mentioned but not verified on a 2026 event page:** ControlMonkey's roundup name-checks parties from MongoDB and Snowflake among the week's best, but no specific MongoDB or Snowflake party (name/date/venue) appeared on the aggregator pages opened, so none is listed. Echelon's FAQ likewise references "Agent Afters" (Superfrico, Wed 8 PM – 12 AM per its schedule) — it does appear in Echelon's own schedule as "Agent Afters – The Best Night Out of AWS re:Invent," but no host or RSVP detail was shown on the pages opened.
- **re:Play performer:** No headline performer for AWS re:Play 2026 was announced on any page opened as of Nov 25, 2026.
- **Counts for context:** Echelon lists 232 events, Reinvent Radar (reinventradar.com) claims 321, Reinvent Radar (.io) lists 42, and Conference Parties lists 67 — the aggregators overlap heavily and none is official. AWS's own official social events (Welcome Reception, Expo Happy Hour, re:Play) require only a badge.
- All details were captured Nov 25, 2026; hosts routinely change venues, times, and capacity, so the RSVP page is the final word before attending.
