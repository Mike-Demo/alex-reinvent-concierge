## 1. The re:Invent campus — six venues

AWS re:Invent 2026 runs across **six venues** on the Las Vegas Strip: **Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, Encore, MGM Grand, The Venetian, and Wynn**. Mandalay Bay and Aria are **not** re:Invent 2026 campus venues.
*Source: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/ ; corroborated by the Attendee Playbook ("six venues along the Strip") — https://builder.aws.com/content/3M1jaspZoTns1i16WgWGvGtrMPy/aws-reinvent-2026-the-attendee-playbook-series-index-start-here*

AWS groups the campus into three clusters:

| Cluster | Venues | What lives there |
|---|---|---|
| **North** | The Venetian / Palazzo, Wynn / Encore | All five keynotes, the Expo and Builder Zone, Venetian Content Hub; Wynn hosts Thursday's security focus day |
| **Center** | Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace | Caesars Forum: Content Hub theaters, Academy rooms, Customer Showcase (Summit Ballroom), Certification Lounge (Academy 407). Caesars Palace: migration, Microsoft and modernization content |
| **South** | MGM Grand | Deep AI/agent workshops (AgentCore, Bedrock), CFM, many 400-level chalk talks |

*Source: https://builder.aws.com/content/3M1jaspZoTns1i16WgWGvGtrMPy/aws-reinvent-2026-the-attendee-playbook-series-index-start-here*

### Caesars Forum
- **Address:** 3911 Koval Lane, Las Vegas, NV 89109 — a dedicated conference center east of the Strip, behind The LINQ Promenade / High Roller, connected by Sky Bridge to Harrah's and The LINQ Hotel.

- ⚠️ **Caesars Forum is NOT Caesars Palace** — they are separate properties, about 0.4 miles apart. Do not confuse Caesars Forum with the Forum Shops at Caesars Palace (3500 S Las Vegas Blvd), which is a shopping mall.
*Sources: Caesars Entertainment brand listings (address); Attendee Playbook (contents, 0.4 mi separation); agenda — https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/agenda/*

### Caesars Palace

## 2. Distances, adjacency & walking

Verified walking/transport estimates between clusters (AWS's own Attendee Playbook labels these "estimates — plan with them, then check the AWS Events app"):

- **The Venetian ↔ Wynn/Encore:** 10–15 min walk
- **The Venetian ↔ Caesars Forum:** 15–20 min
- **Caesars Forum ↔ Caesars Palace:** 5–10 min (0.4 mi apart, different buildings)
- **Center (Caesars) ↔ MGM Grand:** ~20–25 min by shuttle
- **North (Venetian/Wynn) ↔ MGM Grand:** 30+ min by shuttle
- **General AWS guidance:** allow **30–45 minutes** when walking between or within venues; never plan North → South in under 45 minutes.

*Source: https://builder.aws.com/content/3M1jaspZoTns1i16WgWGvGtrMPy/aws-reinvent-2026-the-attendee-playbook-series-index-start-here ; 30–45 min guidance also on https://aws.amazon.com/resources/analyst-summit/*

**Three direct walking routes connect the campus:**
1. **Encore and Wynn are connected internally** (indoors).
2. **A bridge connects Wynn and The Venetian.**
3. **A temporary walkway links Caesars Forum and The Venetian** — available during Expo hours only.

re:Invent ambassadors are stationed throughout the campus for wayfinding help, and the **AWS Events app has interactive maps and wayfinding**.
*Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/resources/analyst-summit/ ; https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/*

## 3. Getting around

### re:Invent campus shuttles (free with badge)
- Shuttles travel between the campus properties and **run continuously during conference hours**. A **valid re:Invent badge is required** to ride.
- **Daily shuttle hours (from the official agenda):**
  - Sun Nov 29: limited service 9:30 AM–8:30 PM (Caesars Forum, MGM Grand, The Venetian)
  - Mon Nov 30: 6:00 AM–9:15 PM
  - Tue Dec 1: 6:00 AM–8:30 PM
  - Wed Dec 2: 6:00 AM–8:30 PM
  - Thu Dec 3: 6:00 AM–5:45 PM; **re:Play shuttle service 6:15–11:00 PM**
  - Fri Dec 4: limited service 6:30 AM–1:00 PM (The Venetian and Wynn | Encore only)
- **Two pairs have NO shuttle service — walk instead:** Wynn ↔ The Venetian (use the bridge) and Caesars Forum ↔ The Venetian (use the temporary walkway during Expo hours). *(Per the AWS Analyst Summit campus-transport page and the Fractivo guide, which notes this was also the case in 2025.)*
- Real-time shuttle travel estimates are in the AWS Events mobile app.
- **Accessible transportation:** contact the transportation supervisor at **+1 619-727-1908**, at least 1 hour before your desired pickup, during shuttle operating hours.
*Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/ ; https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/agenda/ ; https://aws.amazon.com/resources/analyst-summit/ ; https://www.fractivo.com/reinvent*

### Las Vegas Monorail (free with re:Invent badge)
- The Monorail is **free for re:Invent attendees — show your badge** to ride.
- 3.9-mile route on the east side of the Strip, seven stations south → north: **MGM Grand → Horseshoe/Paris → Flamingo/Caesars Palace → Harrah's/The LINQ → Boingo Station at Las Vegas Convention Center → Westgate → SAHARA Las Vegas**. Trains run every ~4–8 minutes.
- **Stations serving the campus:** MGM Grand station (MGM Grand); Flamingo/Caesars Palace station (Caesars Palace); Harrah's/The LINQ station (Caesars Forum, and the closest station for The Venetian). **There is no Monorail station at Wynn/Encore or The Venetian** — walk via the Wynn–Venetian bridge.
- **Modified hours for re:Invent week (posted by the Monorail):** Nov 29 – Dec 2, 2026: 7:00 AM – midnight; Dec 3, 2026: 7:00 AM – 1:00 AM. (Regular hours: Mon 7 AM–midnight, Tue–Thu 7 AM–2 AM, Fri–Sun 7 AM–3 AM.)
*Sources: https://aws.amazon.com/events/reinvent/faqs/ ; https://aws.amazon.com/resources/analyst-summit/ ; https://www.lvmonorail.com/ ; nearest-station mapping per https://www.fractivo.com/reinvent*

### Rideshare & taxi
- Rideshare pickups at the big resorts are in garages, not at the front door: at **MGM Grand, first floor of the self-parking garage**; at **The Venetian, second level of the parking ramp (back wall)**. Pickup spots can move — ask staff.
- Expect surge pricing at session breaks and reception let-out times; taxis queue at main entrances and can be faster/cheaper than a surged rideshare.
- Rideshares between north and south campus venues run roughly $12–$20 at busy times.
*Source: https://www.fractivo.com/reinvent (independent guide — prices are estimates, not official rates)*

### Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) → the Strip
- **Airport address:** 5757 Wayne Newton Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89119. Badge pickup is at **Terminal 1 and Terminal 3**.
- Caesars Forum's brand listing states the venue is **approximately 3.2 miles from Harry Reid International Airport**.
- By road: **~15–25 minutes by rideshare (roughly $20–35, more with surge) or taxi (roughly $25–40)**.
- An official airport-published distance/drive-time figure for "the Strip" generally: **not listed**.
*Sources: Caesars Forum brand listing (3.2 mi); https://www.fractivo.com/reinvent (times/fares)*

### Vegas Loop (optional)
- The underground Vegas Loop has a station at **Encore**; there is no Loop station at The Venetian, Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, MGM Grand, or Allegiant Stadium. Per an independent guide (vegasdailydrop.com, rechecked Nov 16, 2026), the Convention Center & Encore stations are closed Sun Nov 29–Mon Nov 30, then open Tue Dec 1, 7:30 AM–5:30 PM and Wed Dec 2–Fri Dec 4, 7:30 AM–7:00 PM.
*Source: https://vegasdailydrop.com (independent guide — not an official re:Invent source)*

## 4. Key attendee locations

### Badge pickup / registration
Government-issued photo ID required; uploading your photo to the attendee portal in advance speeds pickup. Your badge is required for sessions, shuttles, and the Monorail.

- **Sun Nov 29:** Harry Reid Airport Terminals 1 & 3, 7:00 AM–11:59 PM; MGM Grand and The Venetian, 10:00 AM–8:00 PM.
- **Mon Nov 30:** The Venetian 6:30 AM–5:30 PM; MGM Grand 6:30 AM–9:00 PM; Airport T1 & T3 7:00 AM–11:59 PM.
- **Tue Dec 1:** badge pickup/help desk 6:30 AM–8:00 PM at MGM Grand and The Venetian; 6:30 AM–7:00 PM at Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, and Wynn | Encore.
- MGM Grand and The Venetian keep badge pickup open through Thursday (per Fractivo). Per-day badge-pickup hours for Wed–Fri are **not listed** on the official agenda page (help-desk hours are; see below).
- **Help desks:** Mon 6:30 AM–8:00 PM at Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, Wynn | Encore (Sun: Caesars Forum 10:00 AM–6:00 PM); Tue 6:30 AM–8:00 PM (MGM Grand, The Venetian) and 6:30 AM–7:00 PM (Caesars Forum, Caesars Palace, Wynn | Encore).

### Expo — The Venetian
